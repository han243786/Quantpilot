This child owns private execution and fill-report helper logic:

- map fill/open-order lists to `ExecutionStatus`;
- resolve market state with reference-price fallback;
- compute executable quantity from order type, side, marketability, and liquidity;
- scope execution assumptions from order-level slippage and fee settings;
- reconstruct resting open orders into temporary `SimOrder`s;
- decide marketability and whether orders may rest;
- construct fill reports for immediate and resting-order fills.
